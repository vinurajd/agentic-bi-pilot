# Agentic BI Pilot — Privacy

Agentic BI Pilot is a set of three instruction-only skills for analytics
professionals. It runs inside the AI host the user already uses. The author
operates no server, no database, and no analytics for it.

## What it works with

The skills advise from details the user shares in the conversation about
their own business-intelligence and analytics-agent projects: architectures,
data sources, metrics and their definitions, evaluation results, and
deployment context. The skills do not ask for names, email addresses, or
other personal data, and they do not need any to work.

Everything shared in the conversation is treated as conversation-specific
context, used only to produce the advice in that conversation.

## Storage and retention

The skills store nothing and send nothing to the author or to any third
party. Their outputs (a design brief, an evaluation plan, a readiness
review) are produced in the user's own session and environment, under the
user's control. No data received through these skills is retained by the
author, for any length of time.

## Network access

The skills make no network requests. There is no update service, no
telemetry, and no external connector.

## Who it is for

Agentic BI Pilot is a professional tool for analytics leaders and
practitioners. It is not directed at users under 18.
